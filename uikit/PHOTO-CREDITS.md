## Library demo photos (v0.6, `public/photos/hi/`, Wikimedia Commons) — full attribution (TASL), checked 2026-10-23

Used by the welcome screen, the empty library and the library-with-books demos (`#/screen/first-run`, `#/screen/home`, `onboarding`, `recents-home`). The adapted versions shown in the kit are licensed under the same license as the original: CC BY-SA 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/); c25 is public domain.

Where the credit appears: the covers and backgrounds are demo content, so the screen designs carry **no** credit line. The kit chrome does: under each demo that shows these photos a caption per photo, exactly “Photo: Author · CC BY-SA 4.0 · modified” (author → Commons file page, license → license deed, underline on hover, 11 px #535152), and the full list on the kit page **Photo credits** (`#/credits`, linked from the index footer). `#/full/<id>` renders the bare demo for screenshots; its credits are on `#/credits`.

Modifications (all): resized (c18 to ≤2400 px, others to ≤1400 px) and re-encoded as JPEG q84; cropped by CSS `background-size: cover` with a focal point.
- c18 (welcome): shown in the right ~67 % of the screen, left edge faded into the gray background by a horizontal gradient mask; slow scale/pan animation (1.03→1.09) when reduced motion is off.
- c18b (example cover “Fog”) and the library covers: cropped to the book format (4:5 portrait, 5:4 landscape or square); spine shading gradient over the left edge; overlaid title (Lora italic) and subtitle (spaced caps); page-edge strip and drop shadow; scaled down.
