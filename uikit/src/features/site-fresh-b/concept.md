# site-fresh-b — the row

- **Idea:** the books stand one behind another, cover forward, like records in a crate; you look through them with your hand.
- **Gesture:** grab the front book and pull it aside; it slides out and goes to the back of the row, the next cover is revealed. A flick riffles several; dragging back returns the last one. Inside a book you drag the paper pages the same way.
- **1440:** name and three links on top; on the left the one lead sentence and the index of titles (the front one in ink); on the right the row at true relative size, seen at three quarters so every spine shows, with the front book's title, year, size and pages under it.
- **390:** same order in one column: lead, the row (swipe sideways on the front book, tap a spine), caption, then the index as 48 px rows.
- **1 book:** no row and no index; home is the book page. **12 books:** the row holds all twelve spines, the index groups titles by year.
- **References:** record-shop crate digging (the physical gesture of flicking through a stack); Loose Joints and Nadav Kander (one book as an object on a quiet ground); Bruno Simon / Lusion (the site answers the hand, nothing moves on its own). Web search was not available in this session, so these come from memory, not from a fresh visit.
