# Site fresh C: the programme

- **Idea.** The site runs on the clock, like a broadcast: every minute one book lies open on the table and turns its own pages, then closes and makes room for the next, so every visitor at 14:23 sees the same spread.
- **Signature gesture.** Nothing to learn: the book leafs through itself at a slow, fixed tempo (one book a minute). Touch it, drag a page, press ← / → or Pause, and it is yours; Play goes back to the programme. Reduced motion: it starts paused.
- **1440, first screen.** Name and Books / About / Contact; the lead on the left with "Now, until 14:24", the title, its meta and Pause; the open book on the right at true size with a hairline that fills over the minute; along the bottom the whole programme as standing 3D books with their start times.
- **390, first screen.** Name and Menu; the lead; the open book full width with the hairline; title, meta and Pause; below, the programme as a list: small 3D book, time, title.
- **1 book.** The programme of one: the same open book reads itself every minute, no programme row. **12 books.** The row holds all twelve at a smaller true scale; below it All books grouped by year (on the phone the next four, then the years).
- **Book page.** Same table, no clock: the closed book, title, statement, meta, Open the book, Play to let it read itself.
- **References** (recalled; no web access in this session): Christian Marclay, *The Clock* — time on screen equals real time; Radio Garden — always live, you tune in mid-programme; Nadav Kander and Loose Joints — one book as a physical object on a plain ground, interface in one small size.
